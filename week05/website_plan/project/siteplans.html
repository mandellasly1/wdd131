Future Child Sportive

Empowering children through sports and active lifestyles

Site Name

Future Child Sportive – This name reflects our mission to inspire and support children in sports, encouraging healthy growth and teamwork for the future.

Optional domain availability: futurechildsportive.org

Site Purpose

The website will serve as a hub for parents, coaches, and communities to access information about child-focused sports programs, training resources, and upcoming events. It will also provide registration forms for activities and highlight success stories of young athletes.

Scenarios

  • Where can I register my child for upcoming sports programs?
  • What are the health benefits of enrolling children in regular sporting activities?
  • How can I contact the Future Child Sportive team for partnership opportunities?

Color Schema

Main Colors:

  • Black (#0c0b0b) – Used for headings, header background, and navigation.
  • Black (#0c0b0b) – Used for accents, highlights, and call-to-action buttons.
  • Black (#0c0b0b) – Used for body text.

Typography

  • Verdana – Headings
  • Arial – General body text
  • Georgia – Quotes or highlighted sections
