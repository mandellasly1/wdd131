Future Child Sportive

A site plan for the Future Child Sportive website

Site Name

Site Name: Future Child Sportive

Description: Future Child Sportive is a company dedicated to providing high-quality sports equipment and training programs for children. It's major motive is to give room to all children, especially the less privileged. The name reflects the company's focus on the future of children's sports development and the importance of sportive activities in their growth.

Site Purpose

Purpose: The website will serve as a platform to promote and sell sports equipment, provide information about training programs, and connect with parents and children interested in youth sports.

Services and Information: The site will provide an online store for sports equipment, details about training programs, testimonials from parents, and a contact form for inquiries.

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: What types of sports equipment does Future Child Sportive offer for children.
  • Scenario 2: How can I sign up for a youth sports training program.

Color Schema

  • Primary Color: #2E8B57 (Dark Green) - Used for headings and buttons to convey trust and energy.
  • Secondary Color: #F5F5DC (Beige) - Used for backgrounds and paragraph text for a clean and friendly look.

Typography

  • Primary Font: Arial, sans-serif - Used for body text for readability and a clean look.
  • Secondary Font: Roboto, sans-serif - Used for headings to add a modern and professional feel.

Wireframe

Home Page Layout (Mobile View):

  • Header with logo and navigation menu.
  • Hero section with a call-to-action button.
  • Section for featured products.
  • Section for training programs.
  • Footer with contact information and links.

Home Page Layout (Wide Desktop View):

  • Header with logo and navigation menu.
  • Hero section with a call-to-action button.
  • Side navigation for quick access to sections.
  • Featured products section.
  • Training programs section.
  • Footer with contact information and links.
